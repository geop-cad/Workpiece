# Bearing unit (assembly)

A housing, a shaft turning in two catalogue 6204 bearings, a cover held by four catalogue M5 cap screws: joints, a part pattern, a section through the axis, a parts list and balloons.

Run `python3 examples/bearing_unit/bearing_unit.py` to rebuild `bearing_unit.wpc` (open it in Workpiece), or `python3 examples/build_all.py bearing_unit` to refresh `drawing.pdf`, `drawing.svg`, `thumbnail.png` and `render.png` as well.
